Reach rifle presentation and input/impact update r6

Close the game. Extract this ZIP beside RBDoom3BFG.exe and Play-Reach.cmd.
Merge the reach folders and replace reach/zzzz_reach_switch.pk4 and both
Play-Reach CMD files when prompted.
Launch normally with Play-Reach.cmd, select the rifle in a fresh map,
and check the counter at 32 rounds, after firing, and after reloading.

The r4 forward shift exposed too much of the stock and arms in the owner's
idle screenshot. This update pulls the entire rig 6 units closer to the
camera and puts it farther right/lower: its offset is now 2 forward,
4 right and 2 down. This aims to crop the rear stock and arms against the
viewport edge and place the counter in the lower-right area, guided by the
owner's Xbox360 Reach reference. This remains an approximation: the Reach
and Doom screenshots have different aspect ratios and camera FOVs. It
needs visual comparison in game. World FOV and other weapons are untouched.
The original rig, mesh proportions, imported clips and muzzle joints remain.

The display uses an authored skin remap that omits the shattered-glass
stage. The digit materials now translate before scaling, matching the
engine's matrix multiplication order. The existing reconstructed digit
atlas is reused. Original donor textures and geometry are not in this ZIP.
Fire/reload/sprint/melee and the concrete controller spawn fix are retained.

Play-Reach.cmd now explicitly loads reach_controls.cfg at startup, assigning
Shift sprint and V melee. V is implemented; its latest input path still
needs verification. G quick grenades are not implemented yet.
If V still does nothing, use the included Play-Reach-diagnostic.cmd with
your existing ReachDebugLog.exe, select the rifle, press V twice, then quit
and upload Reach-weapon-debug.log. New traces show trigger/queue/melee stages.

Fast imported bullets previously kept Doom's bullet mass, yielding 50 times
the stock launch momentum. Before the native launch, this update reduces
mass in proportion to speed and disables an additional push. Bullet speed,
damage and Doom's global physics remain unchanged. Check an enemy and a
movable object for excessive shoving; exact reactions need an in-game check.

Send a new idle screenshot showing the counter and the whole weapon view.
If something fails, the existing diagnostic launcher still captures the log.
To revert this framing change, reapply the previous r4 presentation ZIP.
