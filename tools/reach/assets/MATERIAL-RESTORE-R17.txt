REACH r17 - restore the working TACPAD and original-resolution materials

Close Doom. Extract this package beside your existing ReachRBDoom3BFG.exe.
Run Restore-Reach-materials.cmd once, then use Play-Reach-projection.cmd.

The tool reads only your installed r15/r16 PK4s. No donor ZIPs, file pickers,
MCC extraction or new game executable. Keep both existing packs available.
If you already ran r16 Undo, leave its disabled PK4 in the reach folder.

This restores the complete TACPAD material and all four textures byte for
byte from the working r15 pack. Its previous diffuse/blue surfaces, normal,
specular and emissive data are restored together. It also restores the AR,
gloves and suit to their original bump/specular materials and resolutions.

The accepted Steel paint is kept on the two main armor diffuse maps, returned
to their original dimensions. The TACPAD itself uses its previous working
appearance. The r16 PBR/roughness rewrite and 2x texture sets are disabled.

Your currently installed r16 effects are copied unchanged into the new pack.
The old r16 PK4 is disabled with a backup filename. Other packs stay in place.
The two generated effect caches are backed up so the retained effects reload.

The tool does not claim to solve the remaining flat-surface lighting issue.
After checking the TACPAD in a map, hold the AR and run this console command:

    exec reach_material_diagnostics.cfg

This only prints active materials, loaded image information and bump/specular
switch values, then writes Reach-images-r17.log and Reach-materials-r17.log
under the usual RBDoom Saved Games reach folder. No graphics settings change.
Those logs and a screenshot can establish what the running renderer uses.

No model, animation, pose, gameplay script, inventory or engine changes.
Details and source hashes: reach-material-restore17-report.json.

To return fully to r15, remove only the new
zzzzzzzzzzzzz_reach_material_restore_r17.pk4 from reach while Doom is closed;
the r16 pack stays disabled. This also removes the retained Steel overlay.
Restoring r15 effects after that requires refreshing the same two generated
particle caches; keep the r16 Undo tool for that operation.
