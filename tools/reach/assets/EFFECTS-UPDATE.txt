Install the complete r14 combat update first, keeping the TACPAD and older PK4s.
Extract this converter ZIP outside both games.
Double-click Build-Reach-effects.cmd, then select the new
reach-effects-717618e5b2e94d68b4d8007af4608a51.zip you just collected.
Copy the generated reach folder into your Doom 3 BFG game folder.
Start Play-Reach-projection.cmd. No manual exec or new play wrapper is needed.

Adds the actual Reach round/long flash artwork and palettes, the Reach frag
model with normal/specular materials, Reach bounce/explosion sounds and an
explosion assembled from the exported Reach particle textures.
The frag uses the source first-floor-bounce timer (.6-.65 seconds), separate
1.4-second arming minimum, source damage magnitude and radius. The r14 native
extension implements this timer only for the Reach frag.

BFG still supplies physics, lighting, splash falloff and particle rendering.
Throw speed, absolute gravity and particle curves/scales remain approximations.
Missing Blam lighting lookup textures are not needed by these BFG materials.
The accepted AR pose, TACPAD arms, ammo counter and other weapon assets remain.

To undo these visuals/tuning, remove reach/zzzzzzzzzz_reach_effects.pk4.
Converter source is included and available at:
https://github.com/davejohnson884/DOOM-3-BFG/tree/reach-combat/tools/reach/assets
