Extract this complete ZIP into your Doom 3 BFG/RBDoom game folder and overwrite
the previous Reach files. Start Play-Reach-projection.cmd as before.

Start a fresh map for this test: changed weapon script fields can invalidate
older mod saves. The original private Reach PK4 and r8 projection overlay
must remain installed.

V: Reach AR melee animation and one contact damage event.
G: Reach throw animation; consumes one unit of Doom grenade ammo at release.
No grenade ammo means no throw. Normal Doom inventory/pickups are retained.
SHIFT: existing sprint. Rifle pose/projection remains the accepted r12 pose.

Casings now eject toward camera-right with a small upward component, using
the camera basis rather than the imported marker's rotation. Other weapons
retain their released casing code. The executable retains the exact released
RBDoom foundation and prior projection code, with scoped single-player
grenade inventory events added. No threading/display/performance defaults change.

This is a gameplay test, not a completed Reach frag asset port. The frag uses
Doom's grenade model/explosion with provisional timed-bounce tuning. The flash
art is unchanged until the focused Reach export is supplied. TACPAD/material
conversion is separate and does not come in this asset-free ZIP.

Built and checked in CI; actual game playtest required on your PC.
For diagnostics run Play-Reach-projection-diagnostic.cmd.
